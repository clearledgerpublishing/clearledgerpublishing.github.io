Series

Caring For Aging Parents

Practical guidance for adult children facing the real decisions, emotional strain, and family complexity of elder care.

This series is designed for readers who need structure during uncertain seasons: when a parent can no longer live alone, when caregiving must be managed from a distance, when a parent moves in, and when the caregiver is running out of strength.

Series Snapshot

Author

Claire Whitaker

Category

Practical nonfiction, caregiving, family guidance

Best Starting Point

Book 1 for the broadest entry, or choose the title closest to the decision you are facing now.

What Readers Get

Clear structure, emotionally realistic guidance, and practical next steps for difficult family situations.

Who this series is for

Adult children balancing love, responsibility, uncertainty, and exhaustion while trying to make sound decisions for an aging parent.

What makes it useful

Each book centers on a specific caregiving pressure point, so readers can enter the series where they most need help and still get immediate value.

How to use it

Read in order for the strongest overview, or start with the title that matches the current crisis, transition, or season your family is living through.

Reading Order

Start with the book that fits the decision in front of you

The books work as a connected guidance shelf. Book 1 is the clearest broad entry point, but the strongest reader experience often comes from choosing the title that matches the most urgent current challenge.

When Your Parent Can’t Live Alone Anymore

The foundational entry into the series. Built for families confronting the first major shift in elder care, when independence, safety, and daily reality no longer line up cleanly.

Best for: early transition decisions, safety concerns, planning the next step

When You Can’t Be There

Start Here

Choose the book that solves the most immediate problem

Some readers need a broad framework first. Others need help with one painful transition right now. This series is built to support both paths.

If you are unsure where to begin, start with When Your Parent Can’t Live Alone Anymore. If your current problem is more specific, choose the title that most closely matches your actual situation and work outward from there.

Reader Decision Guide

  • Start with Book 1 if you need the broadest overview.
  • Choose Book 3 if distance is the problem.
  • Choose Book 4 if housing and co-living are the problem.
  • Choose Book 6 if your own depletion is the problem.
