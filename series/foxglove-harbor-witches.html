Series

Foxglove Harbor Witches

A paranormal romance series of witches, wolves, harbor fog, secrets, and interconnected love stories.

Foxglove Harbor Witches is built as an immersive series world rather than a loose pile of titles. Each book offers a distinct romantic arc while deepening the setting, expanding the supernatural network around the harbor, and strengthening the reader’s reason to continue through the series.
