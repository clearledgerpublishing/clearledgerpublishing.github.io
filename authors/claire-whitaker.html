Practical, compassionate nonfiction for adult children navigating the real decisions of aging-parent care.

What Claire Whitaker writes

Her books are built for readers who need clarity under pressure. The focus is not abstract theory. It is the lived reality of family caregiving, decision fatigue, and the next concrete step.

Who these books help

Adult children confronting safety, distance, burnout, housing changes, and the emotional strain of trying to do the right thing for a parent while holding together the rest of life.

Why readers stay

The guidance is grounded, organized, and emotionally realistic. These books aim to reduce panic, sharpen judgment, and make hard situations feel more manageable.

Author Bio

Steady guidance for hard family seasons

Claire Whitaker writes for adult children who need practical help, not vague reassurance. Her books speak to the real tension of caregiving: responsibility, uncertainty, guilt, exhaustion, and the pressure to make decisions that affect a parent’s dignity and safety.

Through the Caring For Aging Parents series, she offers accessible, structured support for families navigating the turning points that so often arrive without a clear roadmap.
