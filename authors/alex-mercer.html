Practical AI publishing for professionals and independent firms

Alex Mercer is the pen name of a technologist with a graduate degree and 14 years in the technology sector. His career has focused largely on integrating new technology into small and mid-size businesses in the service economy.

The publishing vision behind the brand is that generative artificial intelligence and increased computing power will materially change modern industry, and that smaller independent firms can use those tools to compete more effectively against much larger, private-equity-backed operators.
