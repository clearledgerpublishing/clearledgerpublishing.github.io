Book Description

A treated marker in brick. A pendant that should not exist. A case that cuts straight into the harbor’s hidden bones.

Nessa Thorne knows the difference between bad luck, bad craft, and deliberate harm. When a kitchen flare at Mill Row reveals a treated marker hidden in the brick, she is pulled deeper into the same widening pattern already destabilizing Foxglove Harbor.

Then Marcus Vale arrives: a wolf detective carrying contraband, case files, and exactly the kind of institutional pressure Nessa has every reason to distrust. He brings a case that reaches beyond the town, but Foxglove Harbor is already too tangled in it to stay untouched.

Founder-marked objects, missing ledger plates, shell companies, and buried route logic all begin to point toward something larger moving beneath the harbor’s visible life. And the more pressure builds, the harder it becomes for Nessa and Marcus to keep distrust from turning into something far more dangerous.

The Apothecary Witch and the Wolf Detective is a darker paranormal romance about secrecy, technical trust, hard-earned respect, and the kind of partnership that becomes unavoidable because both people are too good at the work to stay apart.
