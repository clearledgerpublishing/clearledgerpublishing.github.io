Foxglove Harbor Witches • Book Two

The Apothecary Witch and the Wolf Detective

Arden Ashwood

Paranormal Romance Apothecary Witch Wolf Detective Coastal Investigation Nocturnal Atmosphere Connected Story World

The Setup

Nessa Thorne knows the difference between bad luck, bad craft, and deliberate harm. When a kitchen flare at Mill Row reveals a treated marker hidden in the brick, she is pulled deeper into the same widening pattern already destabilizing Foxglove Harbor.

Then Marcus Vale arrives: a wolf detective carrying contraband, case files, and exactly the kind of institutional pressure Nessa has every reason to distrust. But as founder-marked objects, missing ledger plates, and Blackstone itself begin to point toward a larger extraction effort, distrust is no longer enough to keep them apart.

Where Book Two Goes

Book Two widens Foxglove Harbor from one damaged shop into a larger, buried system. The series becomes more dangerous here, more investigative, and more structurally connected.

Reader Promise

Expect sharper tension, deeper secrecy, stronger procedural pressure, and a romance built as much on precision and trust under fire as on attraction.

Book Description

A colder case. A sharper threat. A darker pull beneath the harbor’s visible life.

Nessa Thorne has no patience for vague danger, sloppy thinking, or men who mistake access for trust. Her apothecary runs on order, precision, and the kind of practical authority that keeps a harbor town alive even when the official version of the story is already failing.

Marcus Vale arrives with a pendant, a file, and a case that reaches far beyond Foxglove Harbor. He is trying to stop a network built on preservation, disguise, and extraction. Nessa is trying to stop her town from becoming evidence in someone else’s system.

Together, they uncover a more dangerous truth: old founder pieces are moving through shell companies, private appraisals, and ordinary paperwork, and the buried weather structure under Foxglove Harbor may be in the process of being stripped for parts.

The Apothecary Witch and the Wolf Detective is a darker, more investigative paranormal romance about pressure, secrecy, technical trust, and the kind of partnership that becomes unavoidable because both people are too good at the work to stay separate.

What to Expect

  • Apothecary craft, residue reading, and founder metalwork
  • Institutional pressure versus local stewardship
  • Sharp, difficult chemistry built on earned respect
  • Coastal smuggling, hidden systems, and route logic
  • A major widening of the Foxglove Harbor world

Series Context

Foxglove Harbor gets larger here.

Book One opens the fracture. Book Two reveals more of the structure under it. This novel deepens the harbor’s hidden logic, raises the scale of the threat, and shows that the problem is no longer contained to one shop or one bad night.
