Book Description

A tower that still answers. A harbor learning it can be used. A keeper who was never meant to hold the whole line alone.

Tamsin Pike knows Blackstone better than anyone in Foxglove Harbor. She knows what the dead public lamp was built to hide, what the sea-side line still refuses, and how much of the town’s safety has always depended on labor inland people prefer not to name correctly.

When sea-side pressure starts testing the tower from offshore, the danger turns public fast. Then Silas Marr is thrown onto Blackstone’s ledge carrying the same route damage now threatening Foxglove, and the crisis shifts from sabotage into something worse.

Silas is not a rescuer. He is a complication: a sea wolf marked by the wrong kind of pressure, carrying knowledge Tamsin cannot ignore and a presence she cannot quite keep outside the tower. As the harbor itself begins reacting in plain sight, the truth becomes unavoidable. What started as theft and fracture is becoming an attempt to force the entire harbor into use.

The Lighthouse Witch and the Sea Wolf is a storm-dark paranormal romance about sea-side custody, buried law, paired labor, and the kind of intimacy that becomes inevitable when two people are finally asked to hold the same line together.
