Foxglove Harbor Witches • Book Three

The Lighthouse Witch and the Sea Wolf

Arden Ashwood

Paranormal Romance Lighthouse Witch Sea Wolf Storm Atmosphere Blackstone Lighthouse High-Stakes Series Expansion

The Setup

Tamsin Pike is the last working sea-side keeper in Foxglove Harbor, whether the town prefers that language or not. Blackstone still answers to her hand, her fire, and the old law the road has spent years flattening into safer words.

When sea-side pressure starts testing the tower from offshore and a wounded outsider named Silas Marr is thrown onto Blackstone’s ledge carrying the same route damage now threatening Foxglove, the problem turns from theft and sabotage into something worse: a forced attempt to make the harbor itself participate.\n

Where Book Three Goes

Book Three takes Foxglove Harbor out to Blackstone and into the sea-side half of the system. The danger becomes more physical, more structural, and more openly public here.

Reader Promise

Expect cliffside pressure, buried keeper law, hard-earned intimacy, and a romance forged under live danger where paired labor becomes as important as desire.

Book Description

A tower that still answers. A harbor learning it can be used. A sea-side keeper who was never meant to hold the whole line alone.

Tamsin Pike knows Blackstone better than anyone in Foxglove Harbor. She knows what the dead public lamp was built to hide, what the sea-side line still refuses, and how much of the town’s safety has been quietly carried by labor no one inland likes to name correctly.

Silas Marr arrives not as a rescuer, but as a complication: a sea wolf marked by a damaged route, carrying the same offshore pressure now trying to find Blackstone’s lawful throat from the wrong side. He knows what it means when old systems stop answering like weather and start answering like instruction.

As the harbor itself begins to react in public, Foxglove can no longer pretend the founder thefts were only thefts. The town’s hidden body is being sounded, measured, and forced toward use. If Blackstone fails, the whole harbor may stop being a wounded system and become an intake.\n

The Lighthouse Witch and the Sea Wolf is a storm-dark paranormal romance about sea-side custody, paired labor, buried infrastructure, and the kind of intimacy that becomes unavoidable when two people are finally asked to hold the same line together.
