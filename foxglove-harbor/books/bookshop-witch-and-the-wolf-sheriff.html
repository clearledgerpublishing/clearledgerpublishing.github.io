Book Description

A waking bookshop. A harbor built on hidden lines. A sheriff who feels like trouble and fate at once.

Ivy Hart returns to Foxglove Harbor for one reason: settle her aunt’s estate, sell the bookshop, and leave before the town gets its hands on her again.

That plan falls apart the moment the shop starts answering her like it remembers her, and Sheriff Rowan Cross shows up with reports of movement, lights, and something hitting the cellar door of a building that should have been empty.

What begins as an inheritance problem turns into something far worse. The harbor’s old protections are under strain. Sabotage is spreading through the town. And the more Ivy tries to stay focused on leaving, the more she is pulled toward Rowan, a man built to hold the line and dangerous precisely because he feels so steady.

As Ivy and Rowan are forced into the same fight, Foxglove Harbor’s hidden structure begins to crack open. To protect the town, Ivy may have to risk the very thing she has spent years trying not to become. To protect Ivy, Rowan may have to step across a threshold that will change everything.
