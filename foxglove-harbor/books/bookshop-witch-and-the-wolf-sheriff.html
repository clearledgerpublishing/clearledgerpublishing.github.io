Foxglove Harbor Witches • Book One

The Bookshop Witch and the Wolf Sheriff

Arden Ashwood

Paranormal Romance Magical Bookshop Protective Sheriff Coastal Atmosphere Slow-Burn Tension Connected Series Starter

The Setup

Ivy Hart comes back to Foxglove Harbor planning to settle her aunt’s estate fast and leave faster. Instead, she finds a bookshop that wakes before she touches the lock, a harbor town hiding older systems beneath ordinary civic life, and Sheriff Rowan Cross waiting on her front step like trouble already knows her name.

What begins as an inherited shop problem turns into something larger: sabotage in the bones of the town, old protections under strain, and a dangerous, undeniable pull between a witch who does not want to stay and a sheriff who was built to hold the line.

Why Start Here

This is the entry point into Foxglove Harbor: the first fracture, the first couple, and the first real look at the harbor’s hidden structure. If you want the strongest series experience, this is where to begin.

Reader Promise

Expect atmosphere, tension, competence, and earned intimacy. This is paranormal romance with a working town underneath it: law, labor, weather, public consequence, and older magic pushing through the practical world.

Book Description

A waking bookshop. A harbor built on hidden lines. A sheriff who feels like fate and danger at once.

Ivy Hart did not come back to Foxglove Harbor to stay. She came to sell the bookshop she inherited, close out her aunt’s affairs, and leave the town to its weather, gossip, and old obligations.

But the shop knows her before she reaches the lock. The bell rings wrong. The threshold answers. And Sheriff Rowan Cross arrives with reports of movement, lights, and something hitting the cellar door of a building that should have been empty.

As Ivy and Rowan are forced into the same fight, the trouble in the shop opens into something bigger than one inheritance or one bad night. Foxglove Harbor’s careful order is starting to crack, and the town’s deepest systems are hidden not in legend, but in the ordinary structures people overlook until they fail.

The Bookshop Witch and the Wolf Sheriff is an atmospheric paranormal romance about old rooms, public consequence, sharpened attraction, and the kind of partnership that becomes dangerous precisely because it works.
