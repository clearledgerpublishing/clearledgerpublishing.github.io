Book Description

Magic, danger, and a man she should not want.

Rowan Vale keeps her magic quiet, her routines tight, and her bookshop exactly the way she wants it: orderly, useful, and safely overlooked. Foxglove Harbor has always survived on hidden balances, old understandings, and the kind of caution people stop noticing until something breaks.

When sabotage begins rippling through the town, Rowan is forced into close contact with Sheriff Elias Thorne, a man too observant, too steady, and far too hard to ignore. He is the kind of danger that does not arrive loudly. He just keeps getting closer.

As the threat surrounding the harbor deepens, Rowan and Elias are pulled into a tangle of secrets, loyalty, and desire. To protect the town, Rowan may have to reveal more of herself than she ever intended. To protect Rowan, Elias may have to step into a world he was never meant to see.

In Foxglove Harbor, magic always asks for something in return. This time, it may ask for everything.

This is the first book in the Foxglove Harbor Witches series and the strongest entry point for new readers. It introduces the town, the tone, and the dangerous romantic atmosphere that defines the books that follow.
